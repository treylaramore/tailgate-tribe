First-timer playbook

Punch, then inside

You don’t need a booster pass, a pop-up, or a cousin who already knows everyone. Pour a cup, catch the Heritage Walk and Skull Session if you want, then head inside for the Noles.

The day

How a Tribe Saturday goes

01

Tent goes up

We raise the inflatable at the published tailgate time. East of Lot 8, NW corner of Pensacola and Varsity.

02

Walk up like you belong

No gate, no list, no handshake password. Say hey. Grab a chair. Stay ten minutes or stay until you head inside.

03

The hang

Punch, tomahawk toss, TVs on other games. New faces, old friends. The Noles play inside Doak — not on our screens.

04

Heritage Walk

Optional. Players walk into Doak from Heritage Fountain, usually a couple of hours before kickoff. Cheer them in, then peel off — we don’t move as a pack.

05

Skull Session, then inside

The Marching Chiefs play at Dick Howser right after the walk — section cheers and a peek at halftime. Then kickoff is in the stadium. See you next home game.

Traditions

How we do gameday

The tent is the landmark

Black inflatable arch, garnet sail, Tailgate Tribe on the front, grassy patch east of Lot 8.

Heritage Walk

Players walk into Doak from Heritage Fountain. Catch it if you want. We don’t expect the tent to empty as a group.

Skull Session

Marching Chiefs at Dick Howser, right after the walk. Then inside for kickoff.

Tomahawk toss

The inflatable axe-throw is part of the tent. Take a turn. Cheer for the kid in front of you.

The War Chant

When it starts, the tent gets loud. If you’re new, watch the people next to you and join in.

The setup

Shade, seats, and a tomahawk toss

Under the tent

What’s waiting

The inflatable canopy

Black arches, a garnet sail, and our spear on the front. Shade, a landmark, and the living room — chairs, couches, and TVs for the rest of gameday. The FSU game itself is inside Doak.

Tomahawk toss

An inflatable axe-throw next to the tent. Kids and grown-ups both get a turn.

BYOB is welcome

Bring whatever you want to drink and keep it for yourself. Nobody expects you to stock the tent. Punch is on the table if you want a cup.

Garnet and gold punch

Two dispensers on the table — one red, one gold. Cups next to them. Help yourself.
